PART 1 — THE PRODUCT, EXPLAINED LIKE A HUMAN
================================================================================

1.1  THE ONE-PARAGRAPH PITCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signalcraft is a free, no-account browser terminal that scores 120 crypto
pairs on real Binance daily data and shows you only the setups that clear an
exclusive quantitative gate: Grade A or better (Quant-Score >= 86), with a
healthy RSI (<= 75) and real 24h dollar volume (>= $10M). Usually that means
the board is quiet — only a handful of Grade-A setups at a time. Every board
signal ships as a finished plan: entry, stop-loss, take-profit targets at a
minimum 1:2 risk-reward, an honest risk badge, and full data provenance.
No ads, no paywall, no registration.

1.2  WHAT YOU SEE WHEN YOU OPEN IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A live ticker with prices from 11 centralized exchanges — Binance, Coinbase,
Kraken, OKX and the rest — scrolling across the top.

Below, the board: only Grade-A setups that passed the gate right now (the
terminal shows how many candidates were rejected today). Each card shows the
pair, direction, Quant-Grade, buying-pressure score (real taker buy/sell
flows, volume momentum, the global Fear & Greed index) and a risk badge.

Click a card for the full dossier: entry, stop, both targets, ATR, SMA 50 /
200, 90d-high distance, 7d/30d performance, score component breakdown, and a
data-as-of stamp. A second click opens the candlestick chart with the trade
levels painted on. One click copies the setup or opens a share card.

Sector filter, risk filter, full-text search, four sort modes, five
languages (DE / EN / ES / AR / ZH), three themes. Phone, laptop, tablet.

1.3  FEATURE LIST, PLAIN ENGLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE
 • Exclusive Grade-A board gate: Quant >= 86 AND RSI(14) <= 75 AND 24h volume
   >= $10M — typically ~10 of 120 scored pairs make the cut
 • Finished trade plans: entry, stop-loss, TP1/TP2, minimum 1:2 R:R
 • Quant-Grade A+ / A / B+ / B / C from real computed indicators
 • Buying-pressure score 0-100: taker buy/sell flows (7d), volume momentum,
   global Fear & Greed — no invented social chatter, no invented on-chain data
 • Rejected-candidate counter: the terminal shows how many setups failed the
   gate today (transparency over fake abundance)
 • Full dossier: ATR, SMA 50/200, distance to 90d high, 7d/30d performance,
   score components, Binance Spot API provenance + data-as-of timestamp
 • Interactive candlestick chart with entry / SL / TP lines
 • 1-2% account-risk position-size calculator in the dossier (e.g. 1% of a
   $10,000 account ≈ $100 risk at the printed stop)
 • DE / EN / ES / AR / ZH with RTL support; three themes
 • No account, no ads, no paywall. Donations only (SOL / BTC / ETH).

NOT IN THE PRODUCT ANYMORE (removed because they were fabricated)
 • No on-chain scores (netflow / whale percentages / DEX-boost pills)
 • No "news & social sentiment" feed with invented catalyst headlines
 • No track-record marketing with overlapping rolling closes — the landing
   track record is a single non-overlapping historical path per published
   Grade-A setup, stops counted as losses, clearly labelled as a tiny
   historical sample and NOT a promise
 • No third-party ad network integration and no ad-block detection gate
